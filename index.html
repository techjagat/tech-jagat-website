Welcome To TECH Jagat

Welcome to TECH Jagat Computer Education, a career-focused computer training institute in Silchar, Assam. With experienced faculty and practical industry knowledge, we help students develop the digital skills needed for today’s changing world. From classroom learning and online learning to modern digital learning, our training combines practical projects, professional computer skills, AI tools, technology and career preparation. Our goal is simple: to help every student learn confidently, develop practical skills, build a strong portfolio and prepare for future career opportunities.
