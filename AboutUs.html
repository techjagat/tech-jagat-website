About Us

TECH Jagat Computer Education is a modern computer education and digital-skills training institute dedicated to preparing students, job seekers, professionals, and aspiring entrepreneurs for a rapidly changing digital world.

We believe that computer education is no longer just an additional skill—it is a foundation for education, employment, business, and personal growth. Our goal is simple: to make quality technology education practical, accessible, career-oriented, and future-ready.

From Computer Fundamentals and MS Office to Advanced Excel, Tally with GST, Web Designing, Graphic Designing, Programming, Digital Marketing, AI Tools, and Artificial Intelligence, we focus on helping learners develop skills they can actually use in the real world.

Whether a student is starting with zero computer knowledge or wants to move towards advanced digital and AI skills, TECH Jagat provides a structured learning environment designed to help them grow with confidence.

Our Mission

To Empower Every Learner with the Skills to Succeed in the Digital Age

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional education and modern industry requirements by providing practical, technology-driven and career-focused education. We aim to:

  • Build strong digital foundations among students and beginners
  • Develop job-ready computer and professional skills through practical learning
  • Introduce learners to the rapidly evolving world of Artificial Intelligence and modern AI tools
  • Help students transform knowledge into real-world skills and career opportunities
  • Encourage innovation, creativity, entrepreneurship, and continuous learning
  • Make quality digital education accessible to learners from different backgrounds
  • Prepare the next generation to confidently participate in the Digital India and AI-powered economy

Our vision is not merely to teach computers. Our vision is to create confident, capable and future-ready digital professionals.

Our Affiliation & Recognition

A Venture of NBCE Skill Development

TECH Jagat Computer Education operates as a Study Centre of NBCE Skill Development, under the National Board of Computer Education (NBCE).

NBCE Skill Development is a national education organisation focused on expanding computer education and skill development opportunities across India. Our association with NBCE provides an important foundation for our commitment to structured and recognised computer education.

Official Website: www.nbceskillindia.in

NBCE Skill Development is:

  • A venture of National Board of Computer Education (NBCE)
  • Registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India
  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified
  • Recognised as one of India's largest education organisations
  • Dedicated to expanding computer education and skill development across India

Verify Our Study Centre: TECH Jagat Computer Education is officially listed online as an NBCE Study Centre. Students and parents can verify our centre through the official NBCE website: NBCE Study Centre Listing
