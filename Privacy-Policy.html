Last Updated: 24 August 2026

Welcome to TECH Jagat Computer Education. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website, services, enquiry forms, course registration facilities, and other digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About TECH Jagat Computer Education

TECH Jagat Computer Education is a computer education and digital-skills training institution providing courses and training in areas such as computer fundamentals, office applications, accounting software, graphic design, web technologies, programming, digital marketing, Artificial Intelligence and other technology-related skills.

TECH Jagat Computer Education is a Study Centre of NBCE Skill Development / National Board of Computer Education (NBCE) and has also been approved as an AI Training Certified Center.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile/WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth or age, where required for course-related purposes
  • Educational qualification
  • Address or locality
  • Course or training programme selected
  • Information submitted through enquiry, registration, admission or contact forms

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may automatically be collected, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Website pages visited
  • Date and time of visit
  • Referring website
  • General website usage information

This information may be used for security, analytics, troubleshooting and improving website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected from you for legitimate business and educational purposes, including:

  • Responding to course enquiries
  • Contacting you regarding courses, admissions and training programmes
  • Processing registrations and applications
  • Providing information about course schedules and fees
  • Managing student records
  • Providing educational and administrative services
  • Sending important course-related notifications
  • Providing information about new courses, workshops and training opportunities
  • Improving our website, courses and student experience
  • Maintaining website security
  • Preventing fraud, misuse or unauthorised activity
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Where appropriate, we may contact you through phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp or email using the contact details you voluntarily provide.

4. Information Submitted Through Forms

If you submit your information through an enquiry, admission, registration, contact or other website form, we may use that information to respond to your request and provide the relevant service.

Please provide accurate information and avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for your enquiry or registration.

5. Course Registration and Student Information

If you register for a course, we may collect additional information required for:

  • Student registration
  • Course administration
  • Examination or certification purposes
  • Attendance and academic records
  • Communication with students
  • Issuing certificates or other course documents
  • Verification or administrative requirements of relevant education/skill-development organisations

Where a course is conducted under or associated with an external organisation, certain information may need to be shared with that organisation when reasonably required for registration, examination, certification or verification.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and user experience.

Cookies may help us:

  • Remember user preferences
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve website performance
  • Analyse website traffic
  • Maintain website security
  • Provide relevant website functionality

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for purposes such as website analytics, communication, hosting, security, payment processing, forms, embedded content or other website functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

8. WhatsApp, Phone, SMS and Email Communication

If you provide your mobile number, WhatsApp number or email address, we may use it to communicate with you regarding:

  • Course enquiries
  • Admission information
  • Class schedules
  • Important notices
  • Registration status
  • Student-related information
  • Educational programmes
  • Relevant offers and announcements

You may request that we stop sending non-essential promotional communications.

9. Payment Information

Where online or digital payment facilities are provided, payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways or financial service providers.

TECH Jagat Computer Education generally does not need to store your complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, CVV or banking password on its website.

Payment information may be processed directly by the relevant payment service provider according to its security practices and privacy policy.

10. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access
  • Unauthorised disclosure
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration
  • Destruction

However, no website, server, online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Therefore, while we make reasonable efforts to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Sharing of Information

We may share personal information only where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, including with:

  • Our authorised staff or administrators
  • Relevant educational or skill-development organisations
  • Course, examination or certification authorities where applicable
  • Technology, hosting or service providers assisting us in operating our website
  • Payment service providers where required to process transactions
  • Government authorities or law-enforcement agencies where legally required
  • Professional advisers or service providers where reasonably necessary

We do not intend to sell, rent or commercially trade your personal information.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including:

  • Course administration
  • Student records
  • Certification and verification
  • Legal and regulatory requirements
  • Accounting and financial records
  • Resolving disputes
  • Maintaining business records

The retention period may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our courses may be available to students of different age groups.

Where a student is a minor, registration or submission of personal information should be completed with the involvement or consent of a parent or legal guardian where required.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission may contact us to request review or deletion, subject to applicable legal and administrative requirements.

14. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including educational organisations, service providers, social media platforms or other resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

15. Social Media

TECH Jagat Computer Education may maintain or link to social media profiles and platforms.

If you interact with us through a social media platform, your interaction may also be governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms of service.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and reasonable verification requirements, you may contact us regarding your personal information and request:

  • Information about the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Updating of your contact details
  • Deletion of information where legally and practically possible
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt-out from non-essential promotional communications

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, certification, examination, contractual or legitimate administrative purposes.

17. Data Breach and Security

In the event of a security incident affecting personal information, we will take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances, including investigating the incident, taking corrective measures and making any notifications required under applicable law.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes in our services
  • Changes to our website
  • Changes in technology
  • Changes in applicable laws or regulations
  • Changes in our privacy practices

When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

19. Consent

By using our website or submitting your information through our forms, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Where consent is required by applicable law, we will seek appropriate consent before processing information for the relevant purpose.
