Q. Who can join this course?

The program is suitable for HSLC/10th-pass students, higher-secondary students, college students, graduates and others who want to develop professional computer and digital skills.

Q. Is this course only for students who want office jobs?

No.

The program provides multiple pathways including office & accounts, digital design & marketing, web & software, and AI-powered digital work.

Q. Do I need previous computer knowledge?

No advanced knowledge is required.

The program begins with computer fundamentals and progressively moves toward advanced and professional skills.

Q. Will students learn AI?

Yes.

AI is a major component of the program, including AI fundamentals, prompting, productivity, content creation, creative workflows and AI-assisted technology work.

Q. Will the AI tools remain the same for 18 months?

The course is designed to be future-ready.

Because AI tools change quickly, TECH Jagat may update the practical toolset while retaining the core AI concepts and workflows.

Q. Will I get a job after completing the course?

No ethical institute should promise a job simply because a student completes a course.

TECH Jagat focuses on skills, practical projects, portfolio development, career guidance, interview preparation and job-readiness.

Actual employment depends on the student's performance, vacancies, selection processes and other factors.

Q. Can I freelance after the course?

The program introduces students to freelancing, portfolio creation, client communication and online work models.

Students can explore freelancing after developing sufficient skills and a suitable portfolio.

Income is not guaranteed.

Q. Can I start my own digital business?

The course introduces students to several possible digital-service and small-business models.

Students can explore opportunities such as design, website services, digital marketing, documentation and AI-assisted digital services.

Q. Will there be practical classes?

Yes.

Practical work is a central component of the program.

Students are expected to complete assignments and projects rather than only attend lectures.

Q. Will I receive a certificate?

Students completing the applicable requirements receive the relevant certification under the institute's NBCE affiliation.

Certification details should be confirmed at admission.

Q. Is this course useful for government-job aspirants?

Computer proficiency, typing, MS Office, documentation and digital skills can be useful for many examination and workplace contexts.

However, this course is not a government-job guarantee or a substitute for preparation for any particular competitive examination.
